Cuppy is designed to be a small, private utility. It does not collect,
transmit, or share any personal data.

## Data Collection

**Cuppy collects no data whatsoever.**

Cuppy does not collect names, email addresses, identifiers, location, contacts,
photos, usage history, or any other personal information. There are no accounts,
sign-in features, forms, or user-generated content.

## Analytics, Observability, and Telemetry

Cuppy contains no analytics, observability, crash-reporting, performance-
monitoring, or telemetry services. Nothing about how you use the app is sent to
the developer or to a third party.

## Local and Cloud Storage

Cuppy does not store user content or personal data locally, in iCloud, in
CloudKit, or on any other remote service. It has no backend and does not sync
anything between devices.

The app's conversion catalogue is bundled with the app. Any measurement
preferences or the most recent conversion choice are app settings only; they
are not personal data, are not accessible to the developer, and never leave the
device.

## Third-Party Services

Cuppy does not use third-party services, advertising SDKs, tracking tools, or
external APIs.

## Changes to This Policy

If Cuppy's data practices change in the future, this policy will be updated
before those changes take effect.
